Case studies

Practice projects built from real research. The business is a made-up local bakery, Sunrise Bakery, Navi Mumbai, so I can show my method honestly. The target pages and sources are real and linked. I don't claim any links as built yet.

Link-building plan
Goal
Plan safe, relevant links for a demo local business.
What I did
Sorted link opportunities into three lanes and set rules from Google's link spam policy: no paid followed links, no heavy link swaps, natural anchor text.
Result
A 3-lane plan with 9 targets and a link tracker. No links claimed as built.
See the full plan

Lane 1: Roundup pages to pitch

  • Top 7 Bakeries in Navi Mumbai to Order from (giftstoindia24x7)
  • Best Bakeries in Mumbai (Curly Tales)
  • Mumbai's best bakeries guide (Homegrown)
  • Breakfast Map Mumbai (The Better India)

Lane 2: Local profiles to claim

  • Tripadvisor bakery listing
  • Justdial listing
  • Sulekha listing

Lane 3: Content worth linking to

  • Idea: a cake size and servings guide
  • Idea: a festival gift box guide

Rules I follow

  • Google treats buying links, excessive link swaps and keyword-stuffed anchors in guest posts as link spam.
  • Any paid or sponsored link must carry rel="sponsored" or nofollow.
  • Anchors: mostly brand name and plain URL, some descriptive words, very few exact keywords.

Tracker columns

Date, target, type, anchor, status, link URL.

Sources: Google spam policies, Google link tagging post.

Citation clean-up
Goal
Make a business's Name, Address and Phone match everywhere.
What I did
Built a priority checklist of listing sites for India and a master record to copy from, plus a list of common mismatches to fix.
Result
An 8-listing checklist in priority order and a NAP master record, ready to run.
See the checklist

NAP master record (demo data)

Sunrise Bakery, Shop 4, Sector 17, Vashi, Navi Mumbai 400703, +91 98XXXXXXXX. This address and number are made up. Every listing copies this exactly.

Listings in priority order

  1. Google Business Profile: the most important local citation.
  2. Bing Places: covers a second map ecosystem.
  3. Apple Business Connect: covers Apple Maps.
  4. Justdial: one of India's most widely used local directories.
  5. Tripadvisor: ranks bakeries for Navi Mumbai.
  6. Foursquare: a free, widely used listing.
  7. Sulekha: strongest for home services and professionals, so check the fit first.
  8. Yelp: free, lower priority for India.

Skipped on purpose

IndiaMART, because it is mainly for B2B businesses. Also generic directory-submission lists: one source notes Google mostly ignores them, and local citations are what still matter.

Mismatches to fix

  • "Shop 4" vs "Shop No. 4"
  • "Sector 17" vs "Sec-17"
  • Phone with and without +91 or spaces
  • Old address still live on one site
  • Keywords added to the business name

Sources: OneCity on NAP consistency, TheGuideX directory list.

Goal
Make my own portfolio easier for Google to understand.

Replaced a plain developer title with a descriptive SEO title, and added a meta description, canonical link, social tags, structured data, one H1 and alt text on every gallery image.

<img src="..." alt="Poster: Traffic Triage, 4 questions and 1 cause">

Why these changes

  • A title should describe the page and avoid repeated boilerplate.
  • Google may use the meta description for the snippet, but it can pick page text instead, so the page text must be good too.
  • Alt text helps people who can't see the image and helps images get indexed.

Source: Search Engine Land on Google's title and snippet guides.

SEO in plain words

What does an off-page SEO executive do?

They build trust for a website outside the site itself: backlinks, directory listings, citations and social profile links, and they record every task.

What makes a backlink good?

Relevance to the topic, a trusted site, real traffic and a natural place in the content. Quality beats quantity.

What is NAP and why does it matter?

NAP is Name, Address and Phone. When it matches everywhere, search engines trust the business more, especially for local searches.

What goes into an ARP report?

The date, the activity, the URL, the anchor text and the status. The exact format changes by company, so I follow whichever one the team uses.
