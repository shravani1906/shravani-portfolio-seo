SEO in plain words

What does an off-page SEO executive do?

They build trust for a website outside the site itself: backlinks, directory listings, citations and social profile links, and they record every task.

What makes a backlink good?

Relevance to the topic, a trusted site, real traffic and a natural place in the content. Quality beats quantity.

What is NAP and why does it matter?

NAP is Name, Address and Phone. When it matches everywhere, search engines trust the business more, especially for local searches.

What goes into an ARP report?

The date, the activity, the URL, the anchor text and the status. The exact format changes by company, so I follow whichever one the team uses.
